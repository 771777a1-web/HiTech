The final validation table (per class, from best.pt) was not recorded in the chats, so MC2 is NOT
measured here. Produce it with:
$ yolo detect val model=$HOME/yolo/runs/fire_rgb/weights/best.pt data=$HOME/yolo/ds_rgb/data.yaml imgsz=320 | tee evidence/<date>_MC2_val.txt

3. Export to the NPU format (Fedora laptop)
$ cd $HOME/yolo/runs/fire_rgb/weights && yolo export model=best.pt format=rknn name=rk3588 imgsz=320 && ls best_rknn_model/
